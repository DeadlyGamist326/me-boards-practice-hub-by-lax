Letter to Folio Snapper

Join two Letter scans into one Folio page (8.5 x 13 in). Everything stays on your device, nothing is uploaded.

1st page: bottom of the folio Scanned upside down, the top is cut off Press Ctrl+V, drop an image, or click to choose
2nd page: top of the folio Upright, the bottom is cut off (sits on top of the 1st) Press Ctrl+V, drop an image, or click to choose
Move with the arrow keys:
←↑↓→ move 1 px, Shift 10 px, 1/2 pick the page, +/- zoom, drag or scroll to pan, Ctrl+wheel zooms at the pointer
