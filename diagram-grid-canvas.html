Tips

Hold Shift while drawing with pen, smooth pen, line or arrow to lock horizontal or vertical. In Select, drag a box over empty space to pick several (Ctrl adds more), drag an object to move it, a corner square to resize (Shift keeps proportions) or a side square to stretch one way. Delete or Backspace cancels a stroke you are still drawing, or removes the selection. With a selection, the color and opacity controls change it. Data box marks where the app puts text; give it a name and a note. Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y redoes, Ctrl+G groups, Ctrl+A selects all.

Shift: straight lines. Ctrl+Z: undo. Delete: remove.
