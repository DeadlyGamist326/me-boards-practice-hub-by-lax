PDF White Box Cover

Cover parts of a PDF with white boxes, then save. Everything runs in your browser; the PDF never leaves your device.

Open a PDF (or drop it here), then drag on any page to draw a white box.
How to use: 1) Open a PDF. 2) Drag on a page to draw a white box; draw as many as you want on any page. 3) Click a box to select it: drag to move, use the blue dots to resize, Delete removes it, arrow keys nudge it (Shift = bigger steps), Ctrl+Z undoes. 4) Press Save PDF. Pages with boxes are flattened into an image with the boxes burned in, so the covered text and pictures cannot be selected or copied. Pages without boxes are kept untouched.
